Investigated the vibration dynamics of a peristaltic pump and flexible-tube system using experimental modal testing and operational vibration analysis. I worked on the dual-accelerometer setup, LabVIEW data acquisition, and frequency-domain analysis used to identify natural frequencies and resonance conditions.

Modal and operational vibration testing

We first used an impact hammer and accelerometers to characterize the system's frequency response. The pump was then operated from 100 to 600 RPM while two accelerometers measured its vibration response through an NI DAQ and LabVIEW.

Frequency response and resonance

Frequency-response and coherence analysis identified dominant natural frequencies near 13.2 Hz, 27.6 Hz, and 45.6 Hz. Operational testing showed amplified vibration when pump excitation frequencies aligned with these modes.

We also compared pump speed with flow rate. Although flow generally increased with RPM, the gain diminished at the highest operating speeds. Because the experiment did not directly establish vibration as the cause, this behavior was treated as a potential mechanical effect requiring further synchronized flow and vibration testing.
