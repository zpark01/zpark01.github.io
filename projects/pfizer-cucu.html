Developed CUCU with a five-person mechanical and electrical engineering team for the inaugural Pfizer Digital Hackathon. The prototype combined NLP-based mood classification with a gamified virtual companion designed to support student mental well-being. The project received 2nd Place.

NLP mood classification

I contributed to the machine-learning and product-development workflow behind CUCU. Journal entries were converted into language representations and processed with a Random Forest classifier to estimate emotional content. The resulting mood profile was then used to generate a visual mood indicator and recommend relevant activities.

Student-facing digital companion

The broader product concept connected the mood classifier to a virtual companion, private journal, mood calendar, wellness activities, mini-games, and school support resources. The goal was to make regular emotional check-ins more engaging while giving students access to relevant support tools.

Prototype demo

Competition outcome

The project received 2nd Place at the inaugural Pfizer Digital Hackathon. Beyond the technical prototype, the team developed the user experience, target deployment strategy, and a roadmap for testing the system within school wellness programs.
