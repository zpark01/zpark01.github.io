About

I'm a mechanical engineer and robotics researcher interested in building intelligent robotic systems that can safely and reliably interact with the physical world.

I'm currently based in Pittsburgh and pursuing an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering - Research at Carnegie Mellon University. I work with Prof. Ding Zhao in the Safe AI Lab , focusing on robot learning and safe physical AI. I am also co-advised by Prof. Jeffrey Ichnowski in the Momentum Robotics Lab , where my research interests include tactile sensing and dexterous manipulation.

Before CMU, I earned a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Bioengineering from The Cooper Union, graduating Summa Cum Laude. My previous work has spanned robotics hardware, medical-device development, mechanical design, sensing, controls, and machine learning.

I'm interested in opportunities to work on challenging problems across robotics, intelligent hardware, and mechanical engineering. Feel free to reach out at zpark@andrew.cmu.edu .
