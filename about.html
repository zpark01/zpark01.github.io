About

I'm a mechanical engineer and robotics researcher interested in building intelligent robotic systems that can safely and reliably interact with the physical world.

I'm currently based in Pittsburgh and pursuing an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering - Research at Carnegie Mellon University. I work with Prof. Ding Zhao in the Safe AI Lab , where my research interests center on robot learning, safe physical AI, and intelligent autonomous systems.

Before CMU, I earned a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Bioengineering from The Cooper Union, graduating Summa Cum Laude. My previous work has ranged from wearable robotics and sensing at HUROTICS, to reusable neurosurgical testbeds at Mount Sinai BioDesign, to uncertainty-aware machine learning for electromagnetic forming at TU Dresden with Fraunhofer IWU. Across these projects, I have worked on mechanical design, prototyping, embedded sensing, controls, experimental validation, and machine learning.

I'm especially interested in problems that combine robotics hardware with learning and sensing, including physical interaction, manipulation, wearable robotics, and systems that must operate reliably in uncertain real-world environments.

I'm interested in opportunities across robotics, intelligent hardware, and mechanical engineering. Feel free to reach out at jhyeonpark01@gmail.com or zpark@andrew.cmu.edu .
